## Jeff Rolling

Product manager working on B2B SaaS for spend management and payments. Amsterdam.

I build workflow software for the unglamorous middle of a company: how a trip gets
booked, how a card gets charged, how a receipt turns into an expense line that
someone has to approve. Five years in product management, four of them owning B2B
SaaS in travel, expense and payments at GetGoing Travel & Expense, a BCD Travel
company, shipped in Germany, the US, the UK, France, Belgium and the Netherlands.

### What I've built

An expense product from zero. GetGoing had no expense offering, so I owned vendor
selection, the API integrations and MVP delivery for one built on AI receipt capture
and partner APIs. It launched as a paid per-user package in August 2025 and went into
four more countries that December, which gave the company a second revenue line.

Checkout and payments for corporate travel. I own the booking flow end to end,
checkout included: bring-your-own-card for Visa, Mastercard, Amex, AirPlus and Amex
BTA, virtual card automation through Conferma, and payment integrations with
Worldline. The surcharge is always disclosed on every payment method, no exceptions,
and I would argue for that decision anywhere.

Booking reliability. When we started, the team's own estimate was that roughly 30% of
bookings were failing. After four months of stabilization work, and measured properly
now, it is under 10%. Time to first booking went from a 49-day average in 2024 to
under 30 days in 2025, and validation of client terms dropped from 64.5 days to 15.3
over the course of 2024.

A nine-month engineering vendor transition, with the outgoing team disengaging and
the incoming team ramping while delivery commitments held. We shipped a Deutsche Bahn
rail integration in the same window.

How the roadmap gets decided. I introduced evidence-based prioritization, which moved
planning away from executive assumption toward customer research and operational
metrics. I also run quarterly enablement for the 31 commercial team leads across
Sales, Ops and Customer Success.

I was the only PM on the product from 2022 to 2025, across five value streams: core
platform and payments, travel, expense, corporate, and the support portal. A second
PM joined in June 2026 and I now own three of the five.
